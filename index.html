What we do

We are a computational lab at the Institute for Anthropological Research in Zagreb. We combine chemical, biological and environmental measurements with machine learning to understand how the places we live in, and what we are exposed to, shape human health.

Exposome and indoor air

Air quality sensors, pollution modelling and indoor environments, and what they mean for health.

Multi-omics

Metabolomics, lipidomics and microbiome data for biomarker discovery.

Machine learning

Interpretable models for prediction, cheminformatics and in silico toxicology.

Clinical and cohort data

Childhood asthma, pregnancy and endometriosis studies with clinical partners.
