INANTRO_Connect.

Building bridges for excellence in multi-omics. A one-year project that helps the Institute for Anthropological Research build international consortia, train its researchers in European project work, and open its multi-omics infrastructure to other groups.

Coordinator · Dr. Mario Lovrić 15 Mar 2026 – 14 Mar 2027 DIGIT · Routes to Synergies

About

01 / Summary

INANTRO is one of the few institutions in Croatia with metabolomics, metagenomics and bioinformatics under one roof. INANTRO_Connect puts that combination to work on European proposals in multi-omics biomarker discovery and digital tools for preventing metabolic syndrome and other non-communicable diseases.

The project combines networking at conferences and brokerage events with preliminary microbiome and metabolome research that strengthens the proposals. It also trains researchers through workshops and secondments and showcases the Institute's laboratories through an open day, a demonstration session and lab tours.

Funded under the Digital, Innovation and Green Technology Project (DIGIT), implemented by the Ministry of Science, Education and Youth and the Croatian Science Foundation and financed through World Bank loan No. 9558-HR.

Objectives

02 / Goals
OB1

Consortia and proposals

Build two international consortia of at least ten partners each, including an SME and a public health organisation, and draft two Horizon Europe proposals, one coordinated by INANTRO.

OB2

Researcher capacity

Train INANTRO researchers in proposal writing, budgeting, project management and open science, so they can lead future international submissions.

OB3

Visible infrastructure

Make the Institute's research infrastructure better known and more widely used, in Croatia and internationally.

Activities

03 / Work
A1

Building consortia

Partner search at three conferences and a brokerage event, partner meetings, and external expert review of the proposal drafts.

A2

Preliminary multi-omics research

A comparison of Illumina and Oxford Nanopore microbiome sequencing, and GC-QTOF methods for biomarker discovery, both run on INANTRO biobank samples and written up as SOPs.

A3

Capacity building

Training abroad for three researchers, secondments at the University of Copenhagen and The Lisbon Council in Brussels, and workshops at INANTRO on innovation, project acquisition and open science.

A4

Showcasing infrastructure

INANTRO_OpenDay, a hands-on demonstration session using the preliminary datasets, lab tours, and a short video on the Institute's facilities.

A5

Project management

Coordination, reporting and communication over the twelve months of the project.

Infrastructure

04 / Facilities

The project builds on the Centre for Applied Bioanthropology, equipped in 2018 with ERDF funding.

Chemical analytics

LC- and GC-QTOF mass spectrometry, ICP-MS/MS with laser ablation.

Molecular anthropology

Illumina MiSeq, Oxford Nanopore PromethION and real-time PCR.

Microbial ecology

Confocal high-content imaging.

Informatics

In-house computing for large datasets, statistics and AI, run by our lab.
